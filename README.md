# 🌸 JANKI AI  
*A blend of Spiritual Wisdom and Scientific Thinking*

JANKI AI is a simple, thoughtful web-based AI project inspired by **Indian spirituality, village wisdom, and modern science**.  
It is built with the idea that **technology should guide, not dominate**, and **AI should feel human, calm, and helpful**.

---

## 🌿 The Story Behind JANKI

In Indian villages, knowledge is shared through **conversation, stories, and lived experience** —  
not complicated systems.

**JANKI** was created with that same spirit.

Just like elders sitting under a tree and answering questions with patience,  
JANKI aims to respond in a **clear, respectful, and meaningful way**.

The name *JANKI* symbolizes:
- 🌼 **Purity of thought**
- 🧠 **Clarity of mind**
- 🔬 **Balance between science and spirituality**

---

## 🔬 Science × 🌸 Spirituality

JANKI believes:
- Science explains **how** things work  
- Spirituality explains **why** they matter  

This project tries to **bridge logic and wisdom**, modern AI ideas with ancient values like:
- Simplicity
- Awareness
- Curiosity
- Inner growth
